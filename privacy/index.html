Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GRW Brands (“we”, “us”) collects, uses and protects personal information when you visit this website or contact us.

It does not cover our online storefronts, which have their own privacy notices, or personal information we process on behalf of clients, which is governed by our agreement with that client.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us. When you email or otherwise contact us: your name, email address, company, and anything you include in your message.
  • Business contact information. Details of contacts at our clients, prospective clients, suppliers and partners, needed to run those business relationships.
  • Technical information. This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. Our hosting provider automatically processes basic technical data — such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested — to deliver the site and keep it secure. Text on this site is displayed using Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google’s servers when a page loads.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiries.
  • To enter into and perform contracts with clients, suppliers and partners.
  • To operate, secure and improve this website.
  • To meet our legal, tax and accounting obligations, and to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

Legal bases (EEA and UK)

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we rely on: performance of a contract, or steps taken at your request before entering one; our legitimate interests in running and developing our business; compliance with legal obligations; and, where required, your consent.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share it only with:

  • service providers that host this website or provide email and other business tools, acting on our instructions;
  • professional advisers, such as accountants and lawyers;
  • authorities, where required by law; and
  • a buyer or successor, in the event of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets.

International transfers

We work with clients and customers in the United States and internationally, so personal information may be processed in countries other than your own. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses.

Retention

We keep personal information only for as long as needed for the purposes above, including to meet legal, tax and accounting requirements.

Your rights

Depending on where you live — including under the GDPR, the UK GDPR and U.S. state privacy laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act — you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, email us at work@grwbrands.com. We will not discriminate against you for exercising them. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children

This website is intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.
